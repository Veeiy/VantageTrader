BRIEF: post-earnings premium scan ("post_earnings"). Time budget: 12 minutes.
First read brief_common.txt in the parent folder. Your folder: post_earnings/ . Output file:
post_earnings/result.json

GOAL: among stocks that reported earnings AFTER THE CLOSE on 2026-11-10 or BEFORE THE OPEN on
2026-11-11, find where the options expiring 2026-11-13 are still priced well above the stock's
normal daily movement (premium still left to deflate today), and measure how much the premium
has already deflated since this morning's open.

STEP 1, universe. preview_scan with filters:
 {"filter_type":"FILTER_TYPE_EARNINGS_DATE","predicate":"PREDICATE_BETWEEN","values":["20261110","20261111"]},
 {"filter_type":"FILTER_TYPE_AVERAGE_OPTIONS_VOLUME","predicate":"PREDICATE_GREATER_THAN_OR_EQUAL","values":["3000"],"interval":"1d","length":30},
 {"filter_type":"FILTER_TYPE_LAST","predicate":"PREDICATE_GREATER_THAN_OR_EQUAL","values":["10"]}
and columns [{"display_name":"IV rank","expression":"ivRank"},{"display_name":"Front IV",
"expression":"frontIV"},{"display_name":"Back IV","expression":"backIV"},{"display_name":
"HV1M","expression":"statVol1Month"}]. If the earnings-date filter is rejected, use an
expression filter "fundamental.earningsYmd" with PREDICATE_BETWEEN and the same values.
For each row call get_earnings_results(symbol) and keep only names whose latest report is
dated 2026-11-10 with timing "pm" or 2026-11-11 with timing "am". Keep at most the 14 with the
highest average options volume. Never TSLA.
STEP 2. For each kept symbol: spot and today's change; the 2026-11-13 expiry must exist (exact
strike nearest spot; none = drop "no_nov13"); quote the K call and put: straddle_mid,
straddle_pct, atm_spread_pct. Drop if a leg has no bid or atm_spread_pct > 0.25 ("spread").
STEP 3. Implied against normal:
 sigma_total = straddle_mid / (0.8 * spot)
 D = 2 + (minutes from now to today's 21:00 UTC close) / 390
 implied_daily = sigma_total / sqrt(D)
 normal_daily = 1.2533 * mean(abs(daily close-to-close move)) over the 20 sessions ending
   2026-11-10 for "am" reporters, and ending 2026-11-10 for "pm" reporters as well (their
   11/10 close is before the report). Use get_equity_historicals interval "day" from
   2026-10-01 (up to 10 symbols per call).
 excess = implied_daily / normal_daily   (above 1 = premium still above normal movement)
 Also today_move = abs(spot / prev_close - 1) and today_range_so_far from 5-minute bars.
STEP 4. Deflation so far today: get_option_historicals 5-minute bars for today for the K call
and K put (same K as step 2), and get_equity_historicals 5-minute bars for the stock. At
09:35, 10:00, 10:30 New York time and at the latest bar give: stock, call, put, straddle,
time_value = straddle - abs(stock - K). Treat an option bar at or under 0.011 as missing.
STEP 5. For symbols with excess >= 1.10 (at most the best 6): wings = listed strikes nearest
K + straddle_mid and K - straddle_mid; quote; credit_mid, credit_natural, width,
max_loss_mid, breakevens, the four option ids, tick size.

OUTPUT result.json: {"as_of_utc","rows":[{"symbol","report":"2026-11-10 pm"|"2026-11-11 am",
"spot","today_move","today_range_so_far","K","straddle_mid","straddle_pct","atm_spread_pct",
"sigma_total","D","implied_daily","normal_daily","excess","path":[{"t","stock","call","put",
"straddle","time_value"}],"wings":{...} or null,"notes"}],"dropped":[...],"errors":[]}
FINAL MESSAGE (nothing else, at most 30 lines): the file path; a table sorted by excess, best
first: SYMBOL report spot today% straddle% implied_daily normal_daily excess spread% and the
time value at 09:35 and now; fly credit and max loss where quoted; dropped symbols by reason;
anything odd in one or two lines.
