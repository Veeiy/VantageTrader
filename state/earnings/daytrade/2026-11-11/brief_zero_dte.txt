BRIEF: same-day-expiry premium scan ("zero_dte"). Time budget: 9 minutes.
First read brief_common.txt in the parent folder. Your folder: zero_dte/ . Output file:
zero_dte/result.json

GOAL: find where options that EXPIRE TODAY (2026-11-11) are priced rich compared with how far
the underlying has actually moved over the same hours of the day recently.

SYMBOLS TO TEST: SPY QQQ IWM DIA GLD SLV TLT USO UNG IBIT NVDA AAPL AMZN META MSFT GOOGL AVGO
AMD PLTR COIN MSTR HOOD SMH XLE XLF EEM FXI KWEB GDX HYG TQQQ SOXL MU NFLX INTC BABA UBER SOFI

STEP 1. get_equity_quotes (20 symbols per call): spot = last_trade_price, prev_close =
adjusted_previous_close.
STEP 2. Same-day expiry? For each symbol query the listed strike nearest spot for expiration
2026-11-11 (try up to 3 plausible strikes on different grids). No contracts = no same-day
expiry: drop with reason "no_0dte". Keep call id and put id of the strike nearest spot (K).
STEP 3. get_option_quotes (20 ids per call) for the K call and put: bid, ask, open_interest,
updated_at. straddle_mid = call mid + put mid; straddle_pct = straddle_mid / spot;
atm_spread_pct = ((call ask - call bid) + (put ask - put bid)) / straddle_mid. Re-quote spot in
the same minute. Record T = the quote time (UTC). Drop if a leg has no bid ("no_bid").
STEP 4. Realized move over the same hours: get_equity_historicals(symbols up to 10 per call,
start_time="2026-10-19T00:00:00Z", interval="30minute", bounds regular). With python, for each
of the last 15 COMPLETED regular sessions before today: P_T = the OPEN of the 30-minute bar
that contains the same New York clock time as T (mind the daylight-time change in
brief_common.txt), P_C = the close of that session's last bar; move = abs(P_C / P_T - 1).
Give avg_move, median_move, max_move, n_sessions. Also today_so_far = abs(spot / today's first
bar open - 1).
STEP 5. cushion_avg = straddle_pct / avg_move; cushion_median = straddle_pct / median_move.
(Above 1 means the premium is bigger than the typical move; a seller wants it well above 1.)
STEP 6. For every symbol with cushion_avg >= 1.0 (at most the best 6): wings = the listed
strikes nearest K + straddle_mid and K - straddle_mid (at least one strike step away). Quote
them. credit_mid = straddle_mid - wing call mid - wing put mid; credit_natural = call bid +
put bid - wing call ask - wing put ask; width = wider wing distance; max_loss_mid =
(width - credit_mid) * 100; breakevens = K -/+ credit_mid. Record all four option ids, the
tick size (min_ticks) and sellout_datetime.

OUTPUT result.json: {"as_of_utc","T_new_york","rows":[{"symbol","spot","K","call":{"id","bid",
"ask","oi"},"put":{...},"straddle_mid","straddle_pct","atm_spread_pct","avg_move",
"median_move","max_move","n_sessions","today_so_far","cushion_avg","cushion_median",
"wings":{...} or null}],"dropped":[{"symbol","reason"}],"errors":[]}
FINAL MESSAGE (nothing else, at most 30 lines): the file path; a table sorted by cushion_avg,
best first: SYMBOL spot K straddle% avg/median/max move cushion_avg cushion_median spread% and,
where quoted, fly credit mid/natural and max loss; the list of symbols with no same-day
expiry; anything odd in one or two lines.
