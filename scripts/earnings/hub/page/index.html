        How these numbers are worked out

        Everything here is the MCP account ending 3413 and nothing else. Results are per trade and after fees, and they count on the day a trade is closed, Pacific time. Deposits and withdrawals are never P/L.

        Open P/L is the credit taken in minus the cost to buy the fly back at the midpoint of each leg's bid and ask. A real close crosses the spread, so it lands a little lower. The second number on the open tile shows that.

        Quotes come live from Robinhood each time you open or refresh this page, and once a minute while the market is open and the page is in front of you. Positions, trades and the notes on the model come from my ledgers, which I upload after every trade and after each daily review.
